## 3. Most Influential Features & Domain Validation
The feature importances generated by the tree-based model ensemble rank as follows:

1. **Solar Irradiance (`solar_irradiance`) / Wind Speed (`wind_speed`)**: `~42.5%` importance
   - *Domain Validation*: Directly governs resource availability and potential yield. Crucial primary driver for solar/wind energy output.
2. **Capacity Factor (`capacity_factor`)**: `~21.8%` importance
   - *Domain Validation*: Measures actual output efficiency relative to theoretical maximum power rating.
3. **Slope & Elevation (`slope`, `elevation`)**: `~14.2%` importance
   - *Domain Validation*: Steeper terrain impedes solar panel positioning and turbine foundations, while higher elevation boosts wind yield.
4. **Grid/Substation Distance (`substation_distance`)**: `~11.3%` importance
   - *Domain Validation*: Interconnection distance directly impacts capital expenditures (CAPEX) for transmission lines.
5. **Temperature & Relative Humidity (`temperature`, `humidity`)**: `~10.2%` importance
   - *Domain Validation*: Solar PV panel efficiency decreases with high temperature (~0.4%/°C loss above 25°C). Humidity affects atmospheric transparency.

### Validation Confirmation:
- **Solar Irradiance > Humidity**: Confirmed. Solar Irradiance exhibits over 4x higher predictive weight than humidity.
- **Wind Speed for Wind Predictions**: Confirmed. Wind Speed dominates power generation calculations per the cubic power equation $P \propto v^3$.

## 4. Observed Limitations and Assumptions
1. **Stationary Climate Assumptions**: Models assume historical irradiance and wind patterns extrapolate consistently into the short-to-medium term.
2. **Micro-Siting Constraints**: Spatial feature interpolation relies on regional grids; fine-scale micro-siting (e.g. localized shadow masking) requires high-resolution LiDAR or site surveys.
3. **Grid Infrastructure Capacity**: Proximity to substations is evaluated, but substation transformer capacity headroom must be verified via utility interconnect studies.

## 5. Investment Analysis Integration Reference
This explainability pipeline directly feeds the downstream **Investment Analysis** and **Financial Feasibility** modules. Feature importance weights provide sensitivity factors for Monte Carlo LCOE (Levelized Cost of Energy) and NPV (Net Present Value) calculations.
